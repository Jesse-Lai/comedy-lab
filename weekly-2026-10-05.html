🎤 Comedy Weekly — October 5–11

Very light week. The only new seed I found in the last 7 days was Jesse’s unfinished setup about people watching short videos at 2x speed. That’s still usable because the premise is clean: life got so fast that even entertainment now feels like a productivity hack.

🧩 What Feels Promising

Why this angle might actually work

💥 Best usable angle this week: redefine 2x speed as the point where convenience becomes self-parody. The engine is simple: short content is already compressed, so speeding it up means we’re no longer consuming content — we’re trying to defeat time itself.

  • The cleanest frame is not “people watch fast,” but “people are so anxious they optimize even tiny pleasures.”
  • This premise is broad enough for any audience because everyone understands short videos, but it still sounds like Jesse: modern life, low-key suffering, and productivity poisoning leisure.
  • The strongest stage version will probably be one crisp analogy instead of a longer rant. This wants to live as fast one-liners, not a big story bit.
