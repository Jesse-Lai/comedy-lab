The International Public Toilet (FULL BIT)

  • Working in a multinational team is like sharing a public bathroom. Everyone shits. Chinese shit, Indian shit, American shit.🔥
  • The Chinese guy finishes shitting, about to flush — Indian guy knocks, comes in, flushes it FOR him. Then runs to the door and yells: "ALL THE SHIT IN THIS BATHROOM? I FLUSHED IT."🔥
  • American hears the commotion, rushes in, takes a deep breath, and goes: "I LOVE this bathroom. For the first time in America... there's FLAVOR."🔥
