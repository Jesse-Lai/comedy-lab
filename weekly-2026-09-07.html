🧩 What Feels Promising

Why these might actually be useful on stage

💥 Best usable angle this week: short “who am I?” lines that merge Jesse’s actual identity — Microsoft, AI, stand-up, Chinese guilt, work-life battle — into one sentence. These are flexible enough for stage intros, portfolio copy, and social bios.

  • “Work-life battle” still looks like the strongest identity line because it is already stage-tested and immediately tells the audience what kind of suffering they’re about to get.
  • The AI lines are good because they make Jesse sound current without becoming generic “tech guy” filler — the joke is not AI itself, but the emotional labor of working with it.
  • “I’m just at a very Chinese time in my life” is still the most distinct voice line in the batch. It feels personal, cultural, and slightly mysterious in a useful way.
