🎤 Comedy Weekly — September 7–13

This week’s material came from one clean pain point: Jesse realized PM interviews don’t reward “the future” nearly as much as they reward “a boring plan that survives follow-up questions.” That tension feels usable because it’s both tech-specific and painfully human.

🔥 Top Premises

Best material worth testing soon

  • I brought product vision to the interview. They were like, “Amazing. Now can your vision fill out a rollout plan?”🔥
  • I learned PMs don’t want to see the future. They want to see next Tuesday with metrics.🔥
  • I pitched too many bold ideas in the interview. Nothing scares adults more than a demo with no implementation plan.🔥

🧩 What Feels Promising

Why this angle might actually work

💥 Best usable angle this week: redefine “vision” as something corporate people claim to want, right until it creates uncertainty. The joke engine is future vs. rollout plan, imagination vs. implementation, demo theater vs. adult accountability.

  • The strongest pattern is not “interview failure,” but the mismatch between what companies say they value and what rooms actually reward.
  • The best lines here are specific enough for tech audiences but broad enough that any adult with a boss can understand the fear of “great idea, now operationalize it.”
  • If Jesse performs these, the tone should be self-own first, industry-own second. That keeps the bit from sounding bitter and makes the audience relax faster.
